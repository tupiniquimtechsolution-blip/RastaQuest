# Cultural Representation Review

This is a release-blocking review for the Rastafari representation and Xangô-inspired iconography/terminology.

## Review scope

- protagonist visual language;
- names, symbols and terminology;
- electrical effects associated with Xangô inspiration;
- dialogue/lore;
- UI/iconography;
- store screenshots, description and promotional copy.

## Required review questions

- Does the game clearly avoid presenting Rastafari and Xangô as the same tradition?
- Are sacred/religious symbols used accurately and respectfully?
- Are stereotypes or caricatures present?
- Does marketing overstate or trivialize religious/cultural meaning?
- Are fictional adaptations clearly contextualized where needed?
- Were reviewer concerns resolved, or explicitly accepted with rationale?

## Evidence

Record reviewer role/context, date, build/content revision, findings, resolution and final acceptance decision. Avoid publishing personal reviewer information unless they consent.

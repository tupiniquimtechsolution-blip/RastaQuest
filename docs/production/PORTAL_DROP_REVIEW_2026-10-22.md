# Portal entry and fallen weapon integration

Continues issues35/36 after main593825f. No production images are assigned to shipping scenes.

## Runtime

Player has a real portal_enter state that stops movement and cancels/blocks combat. The optional portal rim pack supplies a bounded duration (cap0.8s); an unassigned rim preserves zero-delay fallback. Character presentation is explicitly a quiet fade/6-world-unit slide using the existing idle art, not four newly drawn body poses or a final animation claim. Physics/collision scale stays unchanged. Reduced flashes holds the static rim instead of playing the bright keys; character fade remains quiet.

RunRoom latches each entry with a generation token. Damage cancels it; a stale timer cannot resolve a replacement entry. Death takes precedence. Deferred completion grants the normal upgrade choices or victory once, and duplicate selection clears the pending list before advancing. Delayed enemy/boss defeats cannot reopen a portal during death/transition. Rewards/progression never depend on animation completion.

Presenter optionally takes a256px frame-aligned independent weapon layer. On death's second key it creates a nearest/0.5-scale decorative Sprite2D, preserving the exact texture and facing. A downward raycast finds level geometry excluding the player; a short fall aligns the visible alpha bottom to that ground. It sits behind the collapsed body, carries no collider/hitbox and clears on respawn or scene deletion. Source is the existing rigid death_01_weapon export; no redraw, size drift or collision change. Current body death still freezes in air; full airborne-death choreography/long falls remain a visual review risk.
